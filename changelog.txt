Knight Armor and Weapons Changelog
==================================

1.1.1-Fabric-mc1.20.1
---------------------
Fixes
 - Hand Scythe: the cleave no longer hits your own pets, armor stands, teammates or other players when PvP is off
 - Crash when the mod was loaded on Minecraft 1.20.3 or newer, Fabric now shows a clear message instead (supported: 1.20 to 1.20.2)
Changes
 - Throwing axe: the last point of damage is no longer dealt as extra damage that ignores armor (damage is 12 instead of 11 + 1)
1.1.0-Fabric-mc1.20.1
---------------------
New
 - First release for Fabric, with the same content and behavior as the Forge version 1.1.0: Knight armor with its own 3D model, Knight Longsword, Spiked Mace, Hand Scythe and throwing axe. Requires Fabric API
