Knight Armor and Weapons Changelog
==================================

1.1.1-Forge-mc1.20.1
--------------------
Fixes
 - Hand Scythe: the cleave no longer hits your own pets, armor stands, teammates or other players when PvP is off
Changes
 - Throwing axe: the last point of damage is no longer dealt as extra damage that ignores armor (damage is 12 instead of 11 + 1)
1.1.0-Forge-mc1.20.1
--------------------
New
 - Hand Scythe: hits now go through raised shields, but only half of the damage gets through
Fixes
 - The throwing axe could not be crafted. New throwing axe recipe: 2 iron ingots and 3 sticks make 2 throwing axes
Changes
 - Requires Forge 47.3.30 or newer. Older Forge versions crashed at startup, now Forge shows a clear message instead
 - New mod logo
