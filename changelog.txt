Knight Armor and Weapons Changelog
==================================

1.1.0-Forge-mc1.19.2
--------------------
New
 - Hand Scythe: hits now go through raised shields, but only half of the damage gets through
Fixes
 - Knight armor was invisible when worn (missing texture)
 - Crash at startup with Forge versions older than 43.4.19, the mod now works with every Forge version for 1.19.2
 - Removed a debug chat message that appeared on every throwing axe hit
 - Kills by the Hand Scythe cleave showed a raw death message
Changes
 - New throwing axe recipe: 2 iron ingots and 3 sticks make 2 throwing axes (was: iron axe and iron ingot in the smithing table)
 - Thrown throwing axe plays a hit sound and breaks less often when hitting a block (25% instead of 30%)
 - Knight armor, Knight Longsword and Spiked Mace are no longer fire resistant
 - All items are now only in the mod's own creative tab
 - New mod logo
